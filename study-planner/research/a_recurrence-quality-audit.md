## Corrections and precision checks

- Negative critical log exponents are derived, not falsely attributed to a source's weaker nonnegative-exponent statement.
- An actual alternating dyadic quadratic/cubic toll demonstrates failure of root dominance without regularity; a nonalternating example would not demonstrate it.
- Every tight result distinguishes a positive toll equality from an upper recurrence. Compulsory work supplies lower bounds where required.
- The exact floor stopping formula explicitly matches a zero-through-divisor-minus-one base interval.
- The balanced merge comparison proof includes both leaf-depth counts and simultaneous attainability of each recursive worst case.
- Characteristic-root explanations separate repeated nonzero roots, zero last coefficients, cancellation, and signed/complex modes.
- The unperturbed integral theorem states compact-interval boundedness and integrability, nonnegative tolls, bounded positive bases, an input-unit normalization, and interval comparability. Its real-size induction uses consecutive intervals and a finite bounded transition band. No arbitrary perturbation theorem is assumed.
- Native MathML sums, integrals, fractions, limits and function-grouped parentheses avoid stretched function delimiters. Semantic nested superscripts/subscripts and the actual summation operator are used in inline mathematics.
- Laboratory parameter letters and numeric outputs explicitly use the mathematical typeface.
