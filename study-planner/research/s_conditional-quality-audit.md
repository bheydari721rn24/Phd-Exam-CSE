## Audit obligations

- **Definitions and proofs:** Positive conditioning denominators are explicit. The conditioned-measure proof covers countable additivity. Joint-table bounds have endpoint constructions. Chain telescoping retains complete histories. Mutual independence checks all subfamilies. Complement proofs avoid division and preserve boundary cases.
- **Conceptual traps:** Conditional and marginal independence are distinguished in both directions. Complementing information is separated from complementing a target. Truthful reports include their mechanism. Empty random collections remain in all-success/all-zero events. Shared-edge paths are not asserted independent.
- **Solutions:** Every authored problem contains a complete derivation and a transfer explanation rather than only an answer. Authentic stems and option numbering are checked against rendered original PDF pages; answer derivations are explicitly independent.
- **Mathematics and typography:** TeX-like source is converted into native MathML, with STIX Two Math for symbols, scripts, sums, fractions, and diagram labels. Newsreader and Source Sans 3 remain distinct prose fonts. No underlines are introduced.
- **Visual instruction:** Weighted retention, full history, changing urn composition, Simpson weighting, selection, hidden mixtures, reporting, bridge connectivity, and strip limits use explicit saved states. Captions state assumptions. The bridge enumeration distinguishes working and failed edges by solid/dashed lines as well as color.
- **Laboratory:** Integer cells have bounded input, exact normalization, distinct forward/reverse ratios, and exact product independence. A zero row or column reports an undefined conditional rather than a fictitious probability.

## Remaining uncertainty and coverage boundaries

The accessible course pool is bounded to the candidates in the source audit; it is not a global course census. Some candidate documents were unavailable. Source readings are the chapter-relevant written portions, not every lecture of every course. The exercise bank is a curated instructional synthesis rather than republication of every university exercise.

The four authentic examination items do not exhaust all archived probability questions. Difficulty labels are author judgments. Continuous densities are introduced only to clarify the zero-denominator boundary; full multivariate distributions, regular conditional probability, martingales, and stochastic processes are later material. Finite calculations and browser checks do not prove every symbolic assertion or guarantee a score on unseen questions. General claims rely on the explicit proofs and their stated assumptions.
